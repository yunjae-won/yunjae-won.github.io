Yunjae Won

I'm a Ph.D. student at KAIST AI associated with LKLab and STAI. I am co-supervised by Minjoon Seo and Seongjoon Oh. I received my B.S. in Electrical Engineering with a minor in Computer Science from KAIST in 2024. Previously, I interned at Naver Cloud on the HyperCLOVA Pre-Training Team, where I developed a scalable reasoning data filtering pipeline. I also worked at Naver Place, building a distributed hyperparameter optimization framework and a lightweight, high-accuracy visual quality assessment model. Earlier, at the NCSoft Speech AI Lab, I developed a state-of-the-art vocal-track extraction model for K-pop music.

Research

I am primarily interested in preference optimization, continual learning, and large language models. My current research focuses on quantifying information gain to enhance continual learning and knowledge distillation.

Differential Information Distribution: A Bayesian Perspective on Direct Preference Optimization
Yunjae Won, Hyunji Lee, Hyeonbin Hwang, Minjoon Seo
Preprint. Under Review.
arXiv

By interpreting preference optimization as the process of learning differential information, we characterize the structure of preference data, justify DPO's log-ratio reward, and explain the resulting behaviors of trained policies.
